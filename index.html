Privacy Policy

1. Overview

Propai CRM is a Customer Relationship Management system designed for real estate companies. The App is used internally by company employees with different roles and permissions (Admin, Sales Admin, Team Leader, and Sales Representative) to manage clients, track sales, and improve communication between team members.

2. Data We Collect

The App only collects information necessary to operate and manage user accounts within your company’s CRM system. This may include:

  • User full name
  • Email address
  • Profile image
  • Company name
  • User role (Admin, Sales Admin, Team Leader, Sales Rep)
  • Basic activity and interaction data (for internal reporting)

No sensitive data such as precise location, camera, microphone, or contact data is collected or stored.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate users and assign appropriate permissions based on their role.
  • To allow Admins to manage other users and clients in the CRM.
  • To send push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • To improve app performance and stability through Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. Limited technical data may be shared with our trusted service providers only for functionality purposes:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics

5. Account Deletion and Roles System

Each user has specific permissions according to their assigned role within the App:

  • Admin: Has full control, including the ability to create, edit, or delete user accounts and data.
  • Sales Admin / Team Leader / Sales Rep: Have restricted permissions and cannot delete user accounts or sensitive data.

Users who wish to delete their account or data can submit a formal request. Account removal is handled by the Admin of their organization or upon manual review by the Rockaidev support team.

To submit a data deletion or account removal request, please visit: Contact Us Page

6. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as your company maintains an active CRM account. Once your company or Admin requests deletion, your data is securely removed from our servers.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or deletion. However, please note that no digital storage or transmission system is completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected on this page, and the updated date will be revised accordingly.
